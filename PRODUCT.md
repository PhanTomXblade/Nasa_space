# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), React, Tailwind CSS, Motion, and native Web APIs

## Users

School-age students (grades 4-12), science educators, and curious young space enthusiasts seeking an engaging, interactive museum-grade narrative of humanity's off-world hardware legacy.

## Product Purpose

Transform NASA's catalog of dormant and operational planetary equipment into an evocative, interactive educational journey. Illuminate the engineering marvels, scientific discoveries, and quiet perseverance of hardware left on the Moon, Mars, and deep space.

## Positioning

An immersive planetary archive where historic hardware is not viewed as abandoned debris, but as enduring scientific monuments and robotic pioneers that paved humanity's journey across the solar system.

## Operating Context

Classroom discovery lessons, independent student research, museum kiosk displays, and desktop/tablet interactive exploration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interactive narrative structured into cohesive planetary chapters: Lunar Monuments, Martian Pioneers, and Deep Space Voyagers.
- Canvas / frame sequence interactive scrubber utilizing local mission assets (`Sequence/` 50-frame animation sequence).
- Minimum 8 thematic sections featuring at least 4 distinct layout families.
- Zero em-dashes (U+2014) or en-dashes (U+2013) across all content and interface copy.
- Full responsive support with graceful mobile fallbacks and keyboard-navigable interactive modules.

## Brand Commitments

- Official 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge theme: "Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars".
- Factual fidelity to mission names, mission durations, telemetry markers, and historical discoveries.

## Evidence on Hand

- `problem.png`: Official challenge description and mission parameters.
- `Sequence/`: 50 sequential frame captures (`ezgif-frame-001.jpg` through `ezgif-frame-050.jpg`) ready for scrubbing and interactive animation.
- `vid.mp4`: Authentic mission video reference.

## Product Principles

1. Revere the Hardware: Treat each probe, lander, and rover as a heroic robotic explorer that extended human reach.
2. Spark Scientific Wonder: Connect tangible mechanical components (radioisotope generators, solar arrays, sample drills) to monumental planetary insights.
3. Tactile and Engaging: Reward curiosity with interactive scrubbers, mission dossiers, and field telemetry reveals.
4. Editorial Precision: Maintain rigorous factual clarity and clean museum-grade legibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast compliance across all text, status badges, and interactive controls.
- Explicit `prefers-reduced-motion` handling for all sequence scrubbers and scroll reveals.
- Clear, readable typography and content hierarchy structured for school-age comprehension without patronizing tone.
