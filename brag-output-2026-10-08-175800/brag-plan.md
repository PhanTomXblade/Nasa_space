# Brag Plan: Silent Sentinels (Angle 2: The 90-Day Warranty Audit)

## What is this app?
Silent Sentinels is an interactive NASA Space Apps Challenge planetary archive cataloging humanity's dormant robots on the Moon and Mars—presenting the greatest engineering endurance records in human history.

## The angle
"The 90-Day Warranty Teardown."
Consumer tech breaks in two years. In 2004, NASA sent a golf cart to Mars with a 90-day warranty. It drove for 15 years, ran an interplanetary marathon, and never came back. From piano-wire tires to a broken wheel that accidentally discovered ancient hot springs, this angle frames NASA's discarded equipment through deadpan startup/hardware audit energy.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Clean dark grid. Crisp tech font.
"CONSUMER TECH OBSOLETES IN 2 YEARS."
Hard cut:
"NASA GAVE THIS ROBOT A 90-DAY WARRANTY."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Mileage Shock:** "Actual runtime: 5,111 Sols. 45.16 km driven across Mars."
- **The Accidental Discovery:** Spirit Rover's front wheel jammed, dragged through Martian dirt, and uncovered 90% pure volcanic silica hot springs.
- **The Lunar Tires:** Apollo rovers have woven piano-wire tires with titanium chevrons because rubber shatters in the lunar cold—parked facing Earth with 0% rust for 1,000 years.

## Outro / punchline
"THEY NEVER CAME HOME TO CLAIM THEIR WARRANTIES.
TODAY, THEY STAND AS ETERNAL MONUMENTS."
Silent Sentinels: The Interactive Planetary Hardware Archive.
