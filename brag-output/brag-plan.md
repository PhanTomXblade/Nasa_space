# Brag Plan: Silent Sentinels

## What is this app?
Silent Sentinels is an interactive NASA Space Apps Challenge planetary archive and 3D telemetry narrative that revives humanity's dormant robotic scouts left on the Moon and Mars, celebrating them as enduring monuments of human exploration.

## The angle
NASA's discarded rovers and landers are not junk—they are humanity's first interplanetary pioneers. From a 90-day rover that ran a 15-year marathon to a lunar lander whose camera survived space bacteria and was touched by Apollo astronauts, this launch film treats these mechanical scouts with cinematic reverence and scientific awe.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep space void. A green Quindar telemetry pulse flashes across the screen.
"BEFORE HUMANS WALKED ON ALIEN WORLDS..."
A sudden cut:
"ROBOTS WENT FIRST."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Planetary Archive Reveal:** Satellite basemaps and interactive coordinates spanning the Moon and Mars.
- **Opportunity Rover Feat:** Engineered for 90 days, survived 15 years, and ran a 45.16 km marathon on Mars.
- **Apollo LRV & Surveyor 3 Heritage:** Piano-wire tires, 90.2 km lunar traverse, and the only robot ever visited and touched by human hands on another celestial body.

## Outro / punchline
"THEY NEVER CAME HOME.
BUT THEY WILL NEVER BE FORGOTTEN."
Silent Sentinels official emblem, with a call to explore the live Planetary Atlas.

## User flow worth showing
1. Planetary Entry: Pulling away from Earth into the Moon & Mars orbital grid.
2. Coordinate Lock: Telemetry focus on Opportunity (Perseverance Valley) and Surveyor 3 (Ocean of Storms).
3. Field Dossier: Real historical photos, mission odometer milestones, and scientific discoveries.
